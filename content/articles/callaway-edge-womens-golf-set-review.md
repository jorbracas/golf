The Callaway Edge women's set is a **10-piece boxed set**: driver, 3-wood, 5-hybrid, 6–9 irons, pitching wedge, sand wedge and an **Odyssey White Hot Pro putter**, with graphite shafts and headcovers on the woods, hybrid and putter. It's aimed at **beginners and returning golfers** who want a matched, forgiving set from a major brand.

One catch catches buyers out: **there's no bag in the box.** Budget for one.

## What's in the box

| Club | Notes |
|---|---|
| Titanium driver | With headcover |
| 3-wood | With headcover |
| 5-hybrid | Replaces hard-to-hit long irons, with headcover |
| 6, 7, 8, 9 irons | Cavity-back, built for forgiveness |
| Pitching wedge | |
| Sand wedge | |
| Odyssey White Hot Pro putter | With headcover. Many owners rate this the standout club in the set |

The women's version is listed with **graphite shafts**. Lighter shafts help most players generate speed and launch.

## Who it suits

- **New golfers** who want a complete, matched set from day one
- **Returning players** whose old clubs are 15+ years old
- **Gift buyers** who want a recognised brand without a fitting session

It's not ideal for low-handicap players who want to work the ball or tune lofts and shafts. There's no custom fitting, so lengths, lie angles and flex are standard.

## What to check before buying

1. **Height.** Standard women's lengths suit most players around 5'3"–5'8". Taller players sometimes prefer the men's Edge set for extra length; shorter players may want a petite set. If you can, hit both.
2. **The bag.** A lightweight stand bag is the natural partner. See our [women's golf bag guide](/shop/best-golf-bags-for-women) and [lightweight bag picks](/shop/best-ladies-golf-bag-lightweight).
3. **Gaps.** Ten clubs leave room for later additions. A gap or lob wedge is the usual first one, once your short game develops.
4. **Returns.** It's mostly sold through warehouse retailers. Check their return policy in case a driver or wood arrives damaged, which a few buyers have reported.

## How it compares

Callaway's other women's sets (Strata and Reva) cover similar ground. Strata is usually cheaper and simpler, while Reva sets come with a bag and more premium touches. The Edge stands out for value and for the Odyssey putter. If your budget is tight and you're still finding out whether you like golf, a used half set is also a perfectly good way to start.
