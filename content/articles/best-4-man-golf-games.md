The best foursome games keep everyone in it until the 18th. The **scramble** is the friendliest, **best ball** and **Stableford** suit mixed handicaps, and **skins, Wolf and Nassau** add some competition (and small bets). Here are ten formats and how to score each.

## Team formats (2 vs 2 or all four together)

**1. Scramble.** Everyone tees off, the team picks the best shot, and all play their next shot from there. Repeat until holed. It's fast and fun, and the best for mixed abilities. Our [scramble guide](/blog/how-to-play-a-scramble-in-golf) covers handicaps and minimum drive rules.

**2. Best ball (four-ball).** Everyone plays their own ball. The team's score on each hole is the lowest score among the partners. Good for pairs of similar ability. See [how to play best ball](/blog/how-to-play-best-ball-golf).

**3. Shamble.** Pick the best drive like a scramble, then everyone plays their own ball into the hole. Count the best one or two scores. A halfway house between scramble and best ball.

**4. Alternate shot (foursomes).** Partners share one ball and take turns hitting. One tees off on odd holes, the other on even. It's quick, and it rewards partnerships, as at the Ryder Cup.

## Individual or points games

**5. Stableford.** Points per hole: **0** for double bogey or worse, **1** bogey, **2** par, **3** birdie, **4** eagle, using net scores with handicaps. Highest total wins. Blow-up holes barely hurt, which keeps the pace up.

**6. Skins.** Each hole is worth a "skin". Win the hole outright and you take it. If it's tied, the skin carries over to the next hole. Our [skins guide](/blog/how-do-skins-work-in-golf) covers carry-overs and validation.

**7. Bingo Bango Bongo.** Three points per hole: **first on the green** (bingo), **closest to the pin once all are on** (bango) and **first in the hole** (bongo). Great for mixed abilities, since long hitters don't dominate.

## Rotating-partner and betting games

**8. Wolf.** The order rotates and the "wolf" tees off last. After each drive the wolf can pick that player as a partner, or go alone as the "lone wolf" for double points. Strategy plus banter.

**9. Vegas.** A 2-vs-2 game where each team combines its two scores into a two-digit number (a 4 and a 5 make 45). Lower number wins the difference. Swings can be big, so play for small stakes.

**10. Nassau.** Three bets in one: front nine, back nine and the full 18. "Presses" (new side bets when a team is down) keep it lively.

## Which game for your group?

| Group | Try |
|---|---|
| Mixed abilities, social | Scramble, Bingo Bango Bongo, Stableford |
| Two strong pairs | Best ball, alternate shot, Nassau |
| Competitive friends | Skins, Wolf |
| Want to play fast | Scramble, Stableford, alternate shot |
