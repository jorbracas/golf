**The rule of thumb:** longest clubs at the **back** (the top of the bag, nearest the strap or cart), **irons in the middle** in number order, and **wedges and putter at the front**. Everything stays visible, grips don't tangle, and you can pull the club you need without lifting the others.

## Layouts by divider type

**14-way top** (most cart bags): one club per slot. Put the driver and woods in the top row, hybrids and long irons next, then mid and short irons, wedges at the front, and the putter in its own well if there is one. Our [14-way bag guide](/shop/best-14-way-divider-golf-bag) shows popular designs.

**6-way top:**

| Section | Clubs |
|---|---|
| Top (back) | Driver, fairway woods |
| Top middle | Hybrids |
| Middle left | Long irons (4–6) |
| Middle right | Short irons (7–9) |
| Bottom left | Wedges |
| Bottom right | Putter (+ spare wedge) |

For more detail, see our [6-way setup guide](/blog/6-way-golf-bag-setup).

**5-way top:** woods at the top, then long irons, short irons, wedges, and the putter in the front slot.

**4-way top** (many stand bags): woods and hybrids at the top, long irons in one side slot, short irons in the other, and wedges plus putter in the front.

## Stand bags vs cart bags

- **Stand bags:** the "top" faces the strap side, which is your back when you carry the bag. Long clubs go there.
- **Cart bags:** the "top" faces the cart or trolley handle. Long clubs go there so they don't block the pockets.

## Packing the pockets

- **Ball pocket:** 6–12 balls, plus a few in a quick-access pocket
- **Valuables pocket (lined):** phone, keys, wallet, rangefinder
- **Small accessory pocket:** tees, ball markers, divot tool, pencil
- **Apparel pocket:** rain jacket, spare glove, hat
- **Cooler pocket:** water and snacks

Keep the heavy items low and centred so a stand bag stays balanced. Our [golf bag checklist](/shop/golf-bag-checklist) lists what to bring.

## Small habits that help

- **Headcovers** on the woods (and irons if you like) stop clubs chattering and protect the finish.
- **Count to 14** before a competition round. Rule 4.1b limits you to 14 clubs.
- **Clear out the bag monthly.** Broken tees and old scorecards add weight surprisingly fast.
