A golf simulator shed works if you build it **big enough and tall enough from the start**. Aim for **at least 12×20 ft**, with **14×24 ft or more** if you want a seating area, and an **interior ceiling of 10–12 ft**. Budget for the shed itself (**often ~$12,000–$27,000+ for sim-ready builds**) plus insulation, power, flooring and climate control, *then* the simulator.

## Size that actually works

| Dimension | Minimum | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Footprint | ~12 × 20 ft | ~14 × 24 ft or 16 × 30 ft |
| Interior depth (screen to back wall) | ~15 ft | 18–20 ft |
| Interior ceiling height | ~10 ft | ~12 ft |
| Width for hitting | 10–12 ft (one-handed) | 14 ft+ (left and right-handed) |

Measure **inside** dimensions after insulation and wall finishes. Those eat several inches. Put your numbers into our [simulator room calculator](/shop/golf-simulator-room-size) before you order.

## Roof and wall height

Ceiling height is where most sheds fall short. A standard shed with 7–8 ft walls isn't enough for a driver. Look for **tall sidewalls** or a **barn/gambrel or high-pitched roof** with an open ceiling over the hitting area. Remember the projector and any overhead launch monitor have to fit above your swing.

## What the shed needs inside

- **Insulation:** keeps temperatures stable for you, the projector, the PC and the screen.
- **Electrics:** dedicated circuits for the projector, computer, lighting and heating/cooling. Use a licensed electrician.
- **Flooring:** a level, solid base. Rubber gym flooring or turf around a quality [hitting mat](/shop/best-golf-hitting-mat).
- **Climate control:** a mini-split heat pump is a popular choice for year-round use.
- **Lighting:** good, even light helps camera-based launch monitors. Avoid lights that shine onto the screen.
- **Internet:** for software updates and online play.

## Costs to plan for

| Item | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Sim-ready shed structure | ~$12,000–$27,000+ |
| Insulation, electrics, flooring, HVAC | Varies widely, get local quotes |
| Simulator equipment | ~$2,000–$20,000+ |

See our [golf simulator cost guide](/shop/golf-simulator-cost) for the equipment breakdown.

## Permits and practicalities

Permit rules vary by location, size and use, and adding power or heating often triggers inspections. Check with your local building department before ordering, and check setback rules for how close the shed can be to property lines. A proper foundation (concrete pad or engineered base) keeps the floor level, and a level floor matters for accurate launch monitor readings.

## Shed or garage?

If your garage already has the depth and height, converting part of it is usually cheaper. A shed wins when the garage is too low, too full or needed for cars, and it gives you a dedicated space you never have to set up or pack away.
