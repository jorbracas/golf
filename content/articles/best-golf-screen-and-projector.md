The screen and projector have to be chosen **together**. The **screen size and aspect ratio** set the image you need, and the **projector's throw ratio and brightness** decide whether it can fill that image from a safe mounting spot. For most home sims that means a **short-throw projector with 3,000–4,000+ lumens, mounted above and just behind the hitting area**, aimed at a screen of the **same aspect ratio**.

## The four specs that matter

**1. Throw ratio (where the projector can sit).** Throw ratio = distance to screen ÷ image width. **Short-throw** projectors (about 1.0 or less, many 0.5–0.9) fill a big screen from close range. That lets you mount them above the golfer, out of the swing and without casting a shadow.

> Example: a 10 ft wide image with a 0.7 throw ratio needs the lens about 7 ft from the screen.

**2. Brightness (lumens).**

| Screen / room | Suggested brightness |
|---|---|
| Smaller screen, dark room | ~3,000 lumens |
| 10 ft+ wide screen | ~4,000 lumens |
| Large screen or brighter room | ~5,000–6,000 lumens |

**3. Aspect ratio (match your screen).**

| Aspect | Best for |
|---|---|
| **4:3 / near-square** | Compact rooms. More picture where you hit, less wasted width |
| **16:10 / 16:9** | Wider rooms, a more cinematic view, doubles as a home theatre |

A mismatched projector and screen means black bars or a picture spilling onto the enclosure.

**4. Resolution.** 1080p is fine for smaller screens, WUXGA for mid-size and 4K for large screens with premium software.

## Mounting: avoid the two classic mistakes

- **Mounting in front of the golfer:** cheap, but you'll cast a shadow and the projector is in the firing line.
- **Mounting too far back:** needs a long-throw projector and you'll still get shadows.

The usual sweet spot is **on the ceiling, roughly 2–3 ft behind the tee**. Use the manufacturer's throw calculator to confirm image size and offset for your exact model.

## Laser or lamp?

| | Lamp | Laser |
|---|---|---|
| Light source life | ~4,000–6,000 h | ~20,000 h |
| Up-front cost | Lower | Higher |
| Maintenance | Lamp replacements | Minimal |

## Pairing checklist

1. Measure the room and pick the **screen size** ([room calculator](/shop/golf-simulator-room-size)).
2. Choose the **screen material and colour**. Grey helps in rooms with ambient light ([impact screen guide](/shop/best-golf-impact-screen)).
3. Pick a projector whose **aspect ratio matches** the screen.
4. Check its **throw ratio** fills that width from your chosen ceiling position.
5. Pick **brightness** for your screen size and room light.
