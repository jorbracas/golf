A standard 2-seat golf cart is about **92–96 inches long, 47–48 inches wide and 68–71 inches tall** with the roof on. That's roughly 8 ft × 4 ft × 6 ft. A 2+2 with a rear seat is around **108–112 inches long**, and forward-facing 4-seaters stretch to **114–120 inches**.

Exact figures vary by model year, roof, seat kit and especially lift kit. When an inch matters, for a gate, a trailer or a garage door, measure your own cart.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Club Car Precedent** (2-seat) | ~92 in | ~47.5 in | ~70 in | ~1,000 lb |
| **Club Car Onward** (2-seat) | ~92–92.5 in | ~47.5–48.75 in | ~70–71 in | ~1,020 lb |
| **Club Car Onward** (4-pass) | ~108–112 in | ~47.5–48.75 in | ~71.5 in | ~1,150 lb |
| **E-Z-GO TXT** (2-seat) | ~93–95.5 in | ~47–47.5 in | ~70.5 in | ~990 lb |
| **E-Z-GO RXV** (2-seat) | ~93–94 in | ~47–48 in | ~71 in | ~1,020 lb |

Where sources differ, we show the range. Gas versions of the same carts are usually **250–300 lb lighter** because they don't carry a big battery pack. There's more on that in our [golf cart weight guide](/shop/how-much-does-a-golf-cart-weigh).

## What a lift kit changes

A lift adds height and usually width:

- **Height:** a 4–6 in lift puts the roof at roughly 74–80 in. A lifted Onward 4-passenger is listed at about 80 in.
- **Width:** spindle and long-travel kits can widen the track by 2–8 in, and offset wheels add about another inch. A lifted 2-seat Onward is around 52 in wide.
- **Ground clearance:** from about 4 in stock to 8 in or more.

Our [lift kit guide](/shop/best-golf-cart-lift-kits) has the numbers for each kit type.

## Garage, gate and trailer sizing

| You need | 2-seat cart | 4-seat cart | Lifted 2-seat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Garage floor space | ~9 × 5 ft | ~11 × 5 ft | ~9 × 5.5 ft |
| Door / ceiling clearance | 7.5 ft+ | 7.5 ft+ | 7.5–8 ft+ (check roof height) |
| Gate opening | 54–56 in (more with mirrors) | 54–56 in | 60 in+ |
| Trailer deck length | 8 ft | 10 ft | 8 ft |
| Trailer width between fenders | 5 ft | 5 ft | 5.5 ft |

For shared parking, see our [golf cart parking space dimensions](/shop/golf-cart-parking-space-dimensions). On trailers, use a deck rated comfortably above the cart's weight. An electric 4-seater with lithium can top 1,100 lb before passengers or cargo.

## How to measure your own cart

1. **Length:** bumper to bumper, including any rear seat kit or hitch.
2. **Width:** the widest point. That's usually the tyres on lifted carts, or the mirrors if you have them.
3. **Height:** from the ground to the top of the roof, with the tyres at normal pressure.
4. **Turning space:** most carts need a turning circle around 20 ft across (roughly a 10 ft radius). Longer 4- and 6-seaters need more.
