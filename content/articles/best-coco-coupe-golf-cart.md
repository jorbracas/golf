The Coco Coupe is a **retro-styled electric mini car** with doors, round headlights and a two-tone paint scheme, sold through golf cart dealers and online importers as a **golf cart / low-speed vehicle (LSV)**. Listings typically show a **60 V system with a ~1.5 kW motor, 2+2 seating and "up to ~35 miles" of range**, at around **$9,500–$11,000** before shipping.

It's charming, and that's most of the appeal. Buy it for the style, after confirming it's road-legal and that someone near you can fix it.

## Typical listed specs

| Spec | Typical listing |
|---|---|
| System | 60 V electric |
| Motor | ~1,500 W |
| Seats | 2 doors, 4 seats (2+2) |
| Range | up to ~35 miles (terrain and weight dependent) |
| Size | ~94 × 47 × 64 in |
| Brakes | Discs |
| Extras seen | Backup camera, optional A/C and heat |
| Charging | Standard wall-outlet charger |
| Price | ~$9,500–$10,800 listed |

Specs vary between sellers and versions (some listings disagree on capacity and seating), so ask for the exact spec sheet of the vehicle you're buying.

## Is it street legal?

It's marketed as an LSV, but legality depends on the individual vehicle and your state. Before paying, confirm:

1. **A 17-digit VIN** and a manufacturer's certificate of origin
2. **LSV compliance** (FMVSS 500) with lights, mirrors, seat belts, reflectors and a windshield
3. **Your state's rules** on registration, insurance and which roads LSVs may use (typically 35 mph limits or lower)

Without the paperwork it's a private-property golf cart, whatever the listing says. Our [LSV guide](/shop/best-low-speed-vehicle-golf-cart) explains the difference.

## Questions to ask the seller

- **Who services it near me?** Niche imports live or die on local support.
- **Battery type and capacity in kWh**, plus the warranty on the pack. Run the numbers in our [range calculator](/shop/how-far-can-a-golf-cart-go).
- **Parts availability:** body panels, glass, controller, charger.
- **Warranty:** length, what it covers and who honours it.
- **Delivery:** freight cost, assembly and pre-delivery inspection.
