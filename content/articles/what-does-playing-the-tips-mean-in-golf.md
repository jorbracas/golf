"Playing the tips" means playing from the **back tees**, the teeing ground **furthest from each green**, so the course plays at its **longest**. They're often called the championship tees. Colours vary by course (black, blue and gold are common), so check the yardage on the scorecard rather than the colour of the markers.

## Should you play the tips?

Only if your distance suits them. The PGA of America and USGA's **"Tee It Forward"** campaign gives a simple rule:

> **Average driver distance × 28 ≈ the 18-hole course length that suits you.**

| Average driver (total) | Suggested course length |
|---|---|
| 200 yd | ~5,600 yd |
| 225 yd | ~6,300 yd |
| 250 yd | ~7,000 yd |
| 275 yd | ~7,700 yd |

A quick reality check: if you regularly need **more than a 6-iron into par 4s after good drives**, you're probably too far back.

## Why people play from too far back

Ego and habit, mostly. The cost shows up in three ways:

- **Slower rounds.** More shots and more searching for balls. Playing the wrong tees is one of golf's main causes of slow play, and our [round length guide](/shop/how-long-does-a-game-of-golf-last) shows how quickly it adds up.
- **Less fun.** Long irons and hybrids into every green instead of the shots the architect intended.
- **Higher scores.** See what [a good score](/shop/what-is-a-good-golf-score) looks like from the right tees.

## Does it hurt your handicap?

No. Each set of tees has its own **course rating and slope**, so the system adjusts your score for the extra difficulty. A 90 from the tips counts for more than a 90 from the forward tees. You can check this with our [handicap calculator](/shop/golf-handicap-calculator). Many courses also offer **combo tees** (mixing yardages from two sets) that can be posted for handicap purposes.

## When playing the tips makes sense

- Your driver distance supports the yardage (use the ×28 rule)
- The course is quiet and you won't hold up groups behind
- You want to test yourself on a course you know well
- A competition requires it

Otherwise, pick the tees that leave you mid-irons into most par 4s. You'll score better, play faster and enjoy it more.
