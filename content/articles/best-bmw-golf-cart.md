**BMW doesn't make or sell golf carts.** What people usually mean by a "BMW golf cart" is the **Club Car CRU**, a six-seat electric low-speed vehicle **designed by BMW Group Designworks** (BMW's design studio) and **built and sold by Club Car**. It launched in November 2022 at **$29,334**. BMW only did the design work and earns nothing from sales.

## The Club Car CRU in numbers

| Spec | Club Car CRU |
|---|---|
| Designed by | BMW Group Designworks |
| Built by | Club Car (Augusta, Georgia) |
| Launched | November 2022 |
| Starting price | $29,334 |
| Seats | 6 (L-shaped lounge bench and swivelling front seats) |
| Top speed | 25 mph (street-legal LSV) |
| Range | ~15–20 miles |
| Charging | ~14 hours on a standard 110–120 V outlet |
| Motor | 10 hp continuous / 30 hp peak |
| Size | ~155 × 74 × 77 in (L × W × H) |
| Weight | ~2,200 lb |

## What it's for (and what it isn't)

Designworks took its cue from yachts, where people gather in the open rear deck. The CRU's rear is a wrap-around lounge with seats that flip to face each other and a removable centre table. Club Car's research found owners like to drive to a friend's house and *hang out in the cart*, so the CRU is built for that.

That makes it a lifestyle vehicle for resort communities, beach towns and lake houses, **not a golf course cart**. It's much larger and heavier than a normal cart (see our [golf cart dimensions guide](/shop/golf-cart-dimensions)), and its range is modest for the price.

## How it compares with a premium 4-seater

| | Club Car CRU | Premium 4-seat LSV (e.g. Onward 4 Forward) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | ~$29,000+ | ~$15,000–$17,000 |
| Seats | 6, lounge layout | 4, forward-facing |
| Range | ~15–20 miles | Typically longer with lithium options |
| Size | ~155 in long, 74 in wide | ~120 in long, ~49 in wide |
| Best for | Socialising, resort living | Daily neighbourhood transport |

If you mainly want comfortable transport for four, a [forward-facing 4-seater](/shop/best-4-seat-forward-facing-golf-carts) does the job for roughly half the money. The CRU only makes sense if the lounge is the whole point.

## "BMW-style" custom carts

You'll also find custom carts and body kits that borrow car-brand styling: grilles, lights, paint. They aren't BMW products. Judge them like any [aftermarket body kit](/shop/golf-cart-body-kits-bronco): fitment, materials, lighting and who will service the cart. Kits that copy a carmaker's badges or grille can also raise trademark problems, so we'd steer clear of exact replicas.

## Bottom line

There's no BMW golf cart in a BMW showroom. The closest thing is the BMW-designed Club Car CRU, a striking but expensive street-legal lounge on wheels. For most buyers a premium [LSV](/shop/best-low-speed-vehicle-golf-cart) or a well-specified 4-seater is better value.
