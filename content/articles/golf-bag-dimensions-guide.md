Most golf bags are **32–36 inches tall empty** with a **top opening of about 8–10 inches**. Once you load clubs, the total height is closer to **48–50 inches**, because the driver and woods stand 12–14 inches above the top. That's the number that matters for car boots, lockers and garage racks.

## Dimensions by bag type

| Bag type | Height (empty) | Top opening | Weight (empty) | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunday / carry bag** | 33–36 in | ~6–8 in | ~2–5 lb | Short rounds, range, par-3 courses |
| **Stand bag** | 32–35 in | ~8–9 in | ~3.5–7.5 lb | Walking with a carry strap |
| **Cart bag** | ~36 in | ~9–10.5 in | ~6–9 lb | Riding or push trolleys, more pockets |
| **Staff / tour bag** | 36 in+ | up to ~15 in | 10 lb+ | Tour pros and caddies |
| **Travel bag / case** | ~48–52 in long | fits a loaded bag | 6–20 lb | Flying with clubs |

Weights and sizes vary between brands. A lightweight stand bag can come in under 4 lb, while a premium cart bag full of pockets can top 8 lb.

## Loaded height and why it matters

- **Car boots:** measure **diagonally**. Many sedans only take a loaded bag on the diagonal, and small hatchbacks often need the seat folded or the driver taken out.
- **Lockers:** club lockers are usually sized for a standard bag. Staff bags often won't fit.
- **Garage racks:** allow about 50 in of height per bag plus room to lift it out.
- **Push trolleys:** the bag base needs to sit securely in the lower cradle. Cart bags with a "trolley-friendly" base are designed for this. See our [push cart bag picks](/shop/best-golf-bag-for-push-cart).

## Top dividers

The top size limits how many dividers a bag can have:

- **4–6-way tops:** usually on stand bags. Lighter, but clubs share slots.
- **14-way tops:** usually on cart bags. Every club gets its own slot and grips don't tangle. See our [14-way divider bag guide](/shop/best-14-way-divider-golf-bag).

Full-length dividers stop grips catching. Look for them if you carry 14 clubs.

## Flying with golf clubs

- Use a padded or hard **travel case** sized for a loaded bag, usually around 48–52 in long.
- Many airlines count golf clubs as a standard checked bag if you stay **within the weight limit** (often 50 lb / 23 kg), even though they're oversized. Check your airline's sports equipment policy before booking.
- Protect the driver head: remove it if adjustable, or use a stiff arm (a telescoping rod that stands taller than your driver).
