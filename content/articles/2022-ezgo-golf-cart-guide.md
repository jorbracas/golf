A 2022 E-Z-GO is now about four years old. That's often a sweet spot for used buyers: modern features, plenty of life left and a price well below new. Used E-Z-GOs typically keep **about 55–70% of their new price at 3–5 years old**, so expect most 2022 carts to sell for **roughly two-thirds of what they cost new**. Battery type and condition explain most of the difference between two carts that look the same.

## The 2022 E-Z-GO line-up

| Model | Seats | What it is | Power options |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Freedom TXT** | 2 | The classic, simple two-seater | Electric (lead-acid or lithium), gas |
| **Freedom RXV** | 2 | Newer chassis, AC drive, smoother ride | Electric (incl. ELiTE lithium), gas |
| **Liberty** | 4 | All forward-facing seats, compact footprint | ELiTE lithium |
| **Express S4 / S6 / L6** | 4–6 | Personal transport and utility, some lifted | Electric and gas |
| **Street-legal (LSV) versions** | 2–6 | Lights, belts, VIN, 25 mph | Electric |

"ELiTE" is E-Z-GO's lithium battery system. On a used cart, the battery is the single biggest value factor.

## Confirm the year first

Body panels, seats and roofs get swapped all the time, so the look of a cart tells you little. Read the **serial number plate** and decode it. Our guide on [how to tell what year an EZGO is](/blog/how-to-tell-what-year-a-ezgo-golf-cart-is) shows where to find it and how to read it. If the plate is missing or damaged, walk away or negotiate hard.

## What a 2022 is worth

| Age | Typical share of new price |
|---|---|
| 1–2 years | 75–85% |
| 3–5 years (a 2022 today) | **55–70%** |
| 6–8 years | 35–50% |
| 9+ years | 20–35% |

Recent new prices for comparison: TXT about $9,500–$12,500, RXV $11,500–$14,500, Liberty $12,500–$16,500, plus freight. Lithium adds roughly $2,000–$3,500 new, and a healthy lithium pack holds that value much better than a tired lead-acid set. Our [used E-Z-GO value guide](/shop/used-ezgo-golf-cart-value) goes deeper.

## Used 2022 checklist

1. **Battery.** For lithium, ask for the install date, any warranty and the charger model. For lead-acid, check the date codes and do a [battery health test](/blog/how-to-tell-if-a-golf-cart-battery-is-bad). A lead-acid pack near the end of its life is a $1,000+ negotiation point.
2. **Test drive.** Check the hill climb, the brakes (straight stops, no grinding) and the steering (no wander or clunks), and make sure forward and reverse work smoothly.
3. **Charger.** It should match the pack. Lithium needs a lithium-compatible charger.
4. **Frame and underside.** Look for rust or corrosion around the battery tray on lead-acid carts, and cracked suspension mounts on lifted ones.
5. **Modifications.** Lift kits, bigger tyres and speed upgrades are fine if done well, but ask for receipts.
6. **Paperwork.** For LSVs, check the title and registration. For any cart, a bill of sale that includes the serial number.

## Bottom line

A 2022 E-Z-GO with a healthy lithium pack is one of the safer used-cart buys. Expect to pay around two-thirds of new. Check the serial number, test the battery, and let the battery's condition, not the paint or wheels, decide your offer.
