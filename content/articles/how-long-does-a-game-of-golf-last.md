An 18-hole round takes **about 4 hours 10 minutes** for a group of four on a typical public course. That's around **12–14 minutes per hole**. Nine holes take **about 2 hours**. On a quiet weekday a twosome can play 18 in 3–3.5 hours. On a packed Saturday it can stretch towards 5.

## Typical round times

| Group | 9 holes | 18 holes |
|---|---|---|
| Single player | ~1–1.25 h | ~2 h or less |
| Twosome | ~1.5 h | ~3–3.5 h |
| Threesome | ~1.75 h | ~3.5–4 h |
| Foursome | ~2 h | ~4–4.5 h |
| Busy weekend / event | 2.5 h+ | 4.5–5 h+ |

These assume you aren't held up. In practice **the group in front sets your pace**: a fast pair stuck behind a slow four plays at the four's speed.

## What makes a round longer

- **Tee intervals:** 7–8 minutes between groups packs the course tighter than 10–12.
- **Time and day:** weekend mornings are the busiest. Late afternoon and weekday mornings are the quickest.
- **Course design:** long walks from greens to tees, lots of water hazards and tough rough all add time.
- **Skill level:** more shots and more searching for balls. Beginners should play the forward tees and pick up when they reach their maximum score.
- **Formats:** scrambles and shotgun events often take longer than normal rounds.

## Walking or riding?

A cart saves time between shots, but it isn't a guaranteed shortcut. On **cart-path-only** days you walk back and forth to the path with clubs, and many walking foursomes keep pace with riders. Single-rider [golf scooters](/shop/best-one-person-golf-cart) are the quickest way round when the course is open ahead of you.

## How to play faster without rushing

1. **Play "ready golf"** in casual rounds: whoever is ready hits, if it's safe.
2. **Know your yardage early.** A [rangefinder or GPS](/shop/best-golf-rangefinder-under-200-budget-buying-guide) beats pacing off sprinkler heads.
3. **Line up putts while others putt** instead of starting when it's your turn.
4. **Leave bags and carts on the side of the green nearest the next tee.**
5. **Limit ball searches** to the three minutes the Rules allow.
6. **Have a [packed bag](/shop/golf-bag-checklist)** so nobody holds things up looking for tees or a glove.

## Planning your day

Allow about **5–5.5 hours** door to door for 18 holes: arrive 30 minutes early to check in and warm up, then add time for the drive and a drink afterwards. For 9 holes after work, two and a half hours is a safe estimate.
