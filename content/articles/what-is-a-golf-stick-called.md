The "stick" you hit a golf ball with is called a **golf club**. Golfers carry a set of different clubs, up to **14** under the Rules of Golf, because each one hits the ball a different height and distance.

(If you meant the tall stick with a flag on the green, that's the **flagstick**, or "pin".)

## The types of golf clubs

| Club | What it's for | Typical loft |
|---|---|---|
| **Driver** | Longest club, used off the tee on long holes | ~8–12° |
| **Fairway woods** (3-, 5-wood) | Long shots from the tee or fairway | ~15–19° |
| **Hybrids** | Easier-to-hit replacements for long irons | ~17–28° |
| **Irons** (typically 4–9) | Approach shots. Lower number = longer, lower flight | ~20–42° |
| **Wedges** (pitching, gap, sand, lob) | Short approaches, chips, bunker shots | ~44–60°+ |
| **Putter** | Rolling the ball into the hole on the green | ~2–4° |

"Loft" is the angle of the clubface. More loft sends the ball higher and shorter.

## Parts of a golf club

- **Grip:** the rubber handle you hold.
- **Shaft:** the long tube, usually steel for irons and graphite for drivers and woods. Its stiffness is called its **flex**.
- **Clubhead:** the part that hits the ball. Its face has **grooves** on irons and wedges.
- **Hosel:** where the shaft joins the head. Many drivers have an adjustable hosel to change loft.

Drivers have limits too: the clubhead can't exceed 460 cc. There's more in our [driver head size guide](/shop/golf-driver-head-size-guide).

## What a beginner actually needs

You don't need 14 clubs to start. A half set covers almost everything:

- Driver or 3-wood
- 1 hybrid
- 7-iron and 9-iron
- Sand or pitching wedge
- Putter

## Other golf words you'll hear

- **Tee:** the peg you rest the ball on for your first shot on a hole, and also the area you hit from.
- **Fairway:** the short grass between the tee and the green.
- **Green:** the very short grass around the hole, where you putt.
- **Par:** the number of shots a good player is expected to take on a hole.
- **Handicap:** a measure of your ability that lets players of different levels compete. Try our [handicap calculator](/shop/golf-handicap-calculator).
