The right men's golf jacket depends on **the weather you play in**: a **waterproof shell** for rain, a **windshirt** for dry breezy days, or an **insulated/softshell layer** for cold. Whatever the type, it has to let you **turn fully without the sleeves riding up or the fabric rustling at address**. That's what separates a golf jacket from a regular one.

## Three types, three jobs

| Type | Use it when | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Waterproof shell** | Rain, showers | Taped seams, waterproof rating (see below), breathable membrane, quiet face fabric |
| **Windshirt / windbreaker** | Dry, windy, cool | Lightweight, packable, water-resistant finish, stretch |
| **Insulated / softshell** | Cold mornings, winter golf | Warmth without bulk, stretch side panels, wind resistance |

Many golfers get the most use from a **waterproof shell plus a warm mid-layer** (such as a quarter-zip), combined as the weather changes.

## Waterproof ratings in plain terms

Fabric is often rated by how much water pressure it resists, in millimetres:

- **~5,000 mm:** light showers
- **~10,000 mm+:** steady rain for most golfers
- **~20,000 mm+:** sustained heavy rain

Breathability ratings tell you how well sweat escapes. Golf involves walking and swinging, so breathability matters. And no rating helps if the **seams aren't taped**.

## Fit for the swing

1. **Make a full turn in the changing room.** No pulling across the back or shoulders.
2. **Extend your arms.** The sleeves shouldn't ride up past your wrists.
3. **Listen.** Stiff, crinkly fabric is distracting at address and over a putt.
4. **Check the length.** A slightly longer back keeps rain off your waist when you bend over the ball.
5. **Cuffs.** Adjustable or elasticated cuffs that stay clear of your glove.

## Details worth paying for

- Pockets that zip and sit above a carry-bag strap
- A collar that blocks wind without rubbing your chin
- Half-zip or full-zip, depending on how often you take it on and off
- Packability, if it lives in your bag
