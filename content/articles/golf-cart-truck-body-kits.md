A golf cart truck body kit gives your cart a **mini-pickup look**: a new front end plus a rear section styled as a truck bed. Some kits include a **working cargo bed**, while others are mostly cosmetic. Expect body kits to cost about the same as other full bodies, **roughly $750–$2,000+**. A plain bolt-on cargo box or flip-seat bed is cheaper if all you need is to haul.

## Truck body vs cargo bed: pick by what you'll carry

| Option | What you get | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| **Truck-style body kit** | New front cowl and truck-styled rear, sometimes with a usable bed | The look, light hauling |
| **Bolt-on cargo box / utility bed** | Steel or aluminium box on the rear | Tools, mulch, firewood, beach gear |
| **Rear flip seat with cargo bed** | Rear-facing seat that folds flat into a small deck | Mixed passengers and cargo |
| **Factory utility cart** | Purpose-built bed and heavier suspension | Farms, campsites, property work |

If you'll regularly haul real loads, a utility model or a cart that already has a bed (see [electric golf carts with beds](/shop/electric-golf-cart-with-bed)) beats a styling kit.

## Weight limits matter more than looks

A golf cart isn't a truck. Every cart has a **gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR)**: the maximum for the cart, passengers and cargo together. For an electric two-seater that's often around **1,300 lb**, and the cart itself may weigh close to 1,000 lb. Two adults plus a loaded bed can reach the limit quickly. Overloading wears brakes, springs and tyres and makes the cart unstable on slopes.

## Fitment

Like any [body kit](/shop/golf-cart-body-kits-bronco), truck bodies are built for a specific frame:

- Club Car DS, Precedent, Onward
- E-Z-GO TXT, RXV
- Yamaha Drive, Drive2

Match the kit to your **model and year from the serial plate**, not to photos.

## Before you buy

1. **Check the bed's load rating**, and whether it tips or has a tailgate.
2. **Lights.** A truck look without lights looks unfinished, and you'll need them if the cart is street-legal.
3. **Consider a lift.** Most truck builds pair with a modest lift and bigger tyres. Our [lift kit guide](/shop/best-golf-cart-lift-kits) has the fitment details.
4. **Freight and delivery.** Bodies ship on pallets, so inspect them before signing.
5. **Resale.** A tidy truck build appeals to rural and campsite buyers. Extreme styling narrows your market.
