Gas golf carts use a **CVT**: a **drive clutch** on the engine and a **driven clutch** on the axle, linked by a belt. When the cart **revs but barely moves, judders on take-off, struggles on hills or smells of hot rubber**, the problem is almost always the **belt or one of the two clutches**. Check the belt first: it's the cheapest fix. Clutch repairs typically run **$200–$800 installed**.

Electric carts don't have this system. This guide is for gas carts only.

## How the two clutches work

- **Drive (primary) clutch, on the engine:** stays open at idle. As RPM rises, weights push its sheaves together, gripping the belt and pushing it outward. Think of it as shifting into a taller gear.
- **Driven (secondary) clutch, on the axle:** spring-loaded, it opens and closes in response. It also lets the system shift back down when you climb or slow down.

They work as a pair, so a problem in one often shows up as wear on the belt.

## Symptom → likely cause

| Symptom | Most likely |
|---|---|
| Engine revs high, cart moves slowly | Worn or glazed belt, or drive clutch not closing properly |
| Jerky, grabbing take-off | Dirty or worn drive clutch, idle set too high |
| Poor hill climbing | Driven clutch spring or ramp wear, worn belt |
| Slow to pick up after you ease off | Driven clutch sticking (dirt, wear) |
| Rattle or wobble from the engine side | Worn drive clutch bushings or weights |
| Squeal, burning smell, heavy black dust | Slipping belt, misalignment, worn clutch faces |

## Check the belt first

Remove it and look for **cracks, missing chunks, frayed edges or shiny, glazed sides**. A belt that has worn narrow sits lower in the pulleys and slips. Use the correct belt for your model and year. Belts are inexpensive and often fix the problem on their own.

## Cleaning and maintenance

- **Clean the sheaves dry.** Belt dust builds up and causes erratic engagement. Use a brush, compressed air or a residue-free cleaner.
- **Don't oil the belt faces.** Any lubricant where the belt runs causes slipping. Only lubricate parts your service manual names.
- **Check alignment.** Misaligned clutches wear belts fast. Engine swap guides warn that more than about 1/8 inch of misalignment causes vibration and rapid belt wear.
- **Inspect regularly.** A common recommendation is to check the belt and clutches **every six months or ~125 hours**, more often on dusty roads.

## Typical costs

| Job | Cost |
|---|---|
| Inspection / cleaning | $50–$150 |
| Drive belt | $20–$120 DIY, $90–$250 installed |
| Clutch puller tool | $15–$35 |
| Aftermarket clutch | $80–$300 |
| OEM / premium clutch | $250–$600 |

## Upgrades and engine swaps

If you've fitted a bigger engine, the stock clutch usually isn't up to it, which is why [engine swap kits](/shop/best-golf-cart-engine-swap) include a matched clutch. Higher-rated springs and weights can also change how the cart accelerates and climbs. Change one thing at a time and test.
