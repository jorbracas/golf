The Callaway HEX Tour isn't made any more. Callaway's HEX-dimple tour balls (**HX Tour, HEX Black Tour and HEX Chrome**) were all discontinued years ago. If you find a box now, it's old stock or recycled. The current balls that do the same job are **Callaway Chrome Tour** and **Chrome Tour X**, which still use HEX dimples.

Old HEX Tour balls can still be a bargain for practice and casual rounds. Here's what they were, and how to tell whether a find is worth buying.

## The HEX tour-ball family at a glance

| Ball | Era | Construction | Who it suited |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HX Tour** (improved version) | ~2006 | 3-piece: rubber core, ionomer mantle, thin thermoset urethane cover (~0.018 in) | Better players wanting Pro V1-style spin |
| **HEX Black Tour** | 2012–2013 | 4–5 layers with a urethane cover; "spin separation", meaning low driver spin and high wedge spin | Mid- to low-handicappers |
| **HEX Chrome / Chrome+** | 2013 | 4-piece, dual mantle, TPU cover | Faster swingers chasing distance |

The HX Tour went toe-to-toe with the Pro V1x of its day for distance, and the improved version gained a few yards and more wedge spin over the original. The HEX Black Tour reviewed as a solid all-rounder that felt soft on irons but was less consistent around the greens than the best of its rivals.

## What "HEX" means

Instead of round dimples, Callaway uses a pattern of **hexagons and pentagons**. The ridges between them cover more of the ball's surface with dimple, which Callaway says reduces drag and keeps the flight more stable, especially in wind. The idea survived even after the HEX names were retired: it's still on the Chrome balls today.

## Should you buy old or recycled HEX Tour balls?

**Yes, for practice and casual rounds**, provided they look clean. Things to check:

- **Cover condition:** no cuts or scuffs. Even a small cut changes spin and flight.
- **Colour:** heavy yellowing suggests long exposure to sun or water.
- **Storage:** lake balls that sat underwater for a long time can lose distance. Recycled "mint" or "5A" grades are your safest bet.

For competition golf or if you want the same ball every round, buy a current model: you can't buy a dozen identical HEX Tours.

## Current Callaway equivalents

| If you liked… | Try… | Why |
|---|---|---|
| HX Tour / HEX Black Tour (tour spin and feel) | **Chrome Tour** | 4-piece, urethane-type cover, mid compression, priced against Pro V1 |
| A higher-spinning, firmer tour ball | **Chrome Tour X** | Callaway's highest-spin, highest-speed tour ball |
| HEX Chrome but softer, slower swing | **Chrome Soft** | 3-piece, softer feel, suits moderate swing speeds |
