A 4-seat forward-facing golf cart has two rows that both face the front, so nobody in the back rides looking at where they've been. On new carts expect to pay about **$10,700–$17,000**. The best-known models are the **Club Car Onward 4 Forward** and the **E-Z-GO Liberty 4F**.

If you mostly carry two people and occasionally four, a cheaper 2+2 cart with a rear-facing flip seat may suit you better. The forward-facing layout pays off when the back seat is in use most of the time, for example for families, in 55+ communities or for trips to the beach.

## Models worth looking at

| Model | Starting price (new) | Length | Top speed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Club Car Onward 4 Forward LSV | $14,999 ($16,999 Lithium XR) | ~120 in | 25 mph | Street-legal LSV from the factory, lithium, three drive modes |
| E-Z-GO Liberty 4F | $13,499 (X), $15,799 (XT), $16,299 (XTL) | ~119 in | 19 mph | ELiTE lithium; XTL can be configured as an LSV |
| Evolution D5 Ranger 4 Plus | ~$10,699 | ~115 in | 19 mph | Shorter but much wider (~56 in) |

Prices are manufacturer list prices at the time of writing. Dealers add freight and prep, and street-legal packages cost extra.

## Forward-facing vs rear-flip seat: the real trade-offs

**Comfort.** This is the whole point. Passengers face forward, can talk to the driver and don't feel every bump while riding backwards. With kids or older passengers you'll notice the difference.

**Length and turning.** Forward-facing carts need a longer wheelbase, so they're typically 9.5–10 ft long against roughly 9 ft for a 2+2 with a flip seat. Check the turning space on your driveway and in the golf cart parking at your community. Our [parking space dimensions guide](/shop/golf-cart-parking-space-dimensions) has the numbers.

**Cargo.** A rear-flip seat turns into a small bed for coolers, clubs or mulch. A forward-facing cart gives you a second bench and a small trunk at most.

**Price.** The same brand's 2+2 model usually costs less, and used rear-seat carts are far easier to find.

## What to check before you buy

1. **Golf cart or LSV?** A low-speed vehicle (LSV) is built for public roads up to 25 mph, with lights, mirrors, seat belts and a VIN. A golf cart configuration is limited to private roads and paths where carts are allowed. Our [LSV guide](/shop/best-low-speed-vehicle-golf-cart) explains the difference.
2. **Battery and range.** Every current premium forward-facing model is lithium. Ask for the pack size in kWh, then run it through our [range calculator](/shop/how-far-can-a-golf-cart-go). Four adults and hills can cut range by a third.
3. **Width, not just length.** Some value models are noticeably wider. Measure gates and the garage door.
4. **Seat belts in the back.** Make sure every seat has one, ideally three-point belts on LSVs.
5. **Service.** A dealer within driving distance matters more than a long spec sheet. Lithium packs and controllers are dealer items.

## Bottom line

If four people ride most of the time, the forward-facing layout is worth the extra length and money. Club Car's Onward 4 Forward is the one to buy if you want a factory street-legal cart. E-Z-GO's Liberty 4F costs a little less to start with, with a lot of features. If the back seat is mainly for groceries, save the money and get a 2+2.
