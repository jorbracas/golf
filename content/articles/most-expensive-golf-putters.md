The most expensive putter ever sold is a **Tiger Woods backup Scotty Cameron Newport 2**, which went for **$393,300 at auction in 2021**. Other Tiger backups have fetched **$328,576** and **$154,928**. Those prices come from who used the putter, not how it rolls the ball. For everyone else, "expensive" means **tour-issue Scotty Camerons at $5,000–$40,000** and premium retail putters at a few hundred dollars.

## Auction records

| Putter | Price | Year | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiger Woods backup Scotty Cameron Newport 2 | **$393,300** | 2021 | Twin of his famous gamer, Tiger provenance |
| Tiger Woods backup Newport 2 | $328,576 | 2022 | Same story, another backup |
| Tiger Woods backup Newport 2 | $154,928 | 2020 | Record at the time |
| Bernhard Langer Scotty Cameron Classic I (1993 Masters) | ~$59,098 | — | Major-winning putter, historic for the brand |

An appraiser quoted by GOLF.com estimated Tiger's actual competition putter would be worth **$3–5 million** if it ever came up for sale.

## Collector putters

| Type | Typical resale |
|---|---|
| Scotty Cameron **Circle T** (tour-only stamp) | ~$5,000–$40,000 |
| Limited-edition tour replicas (e.g. Tiger "Stainless" LE) | up to ~$20,000 |
| "Tour Only" special models | ~$6,000–$9,500 |

**Circle T** putters are made for tour players, not retail, so they're rare. Provenance (whose bag it came from) and condition drive the price. Fakes exist, so buy through reputable dealers with documentation.

## Expensive putters you can actually buy

At retail, premium putters (milled faces, multi-material heads, custom options) typically run from a few hundred to around a thousand dollars, and custom-shop builds can cost more. You're paying for materials, finish and fitting options. They roll the ball as well as your stroke allows.

## Will a pricier putter help?

Only if it **fits**. The things that change your putting:

1. **Length and lie** that let your eyes sit over the ball
2. **Head shape** that suits your stroke: blades for an arc, mallets for straighter strokes
3. **Alignment aids** you can actually trust
4. **Practice.** A [putting mat](/shop/best-primeputt-golf-putting-mat) and our [putting guide](/blog/golf-how-to-putt-ultimate-guide) will lower your scores far more than an auction-grade putter.
