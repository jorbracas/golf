**To start most golf carts:** unplug the charger (electric), set **Tow/Run to Run**, sit in the driver's seat, **hold the brake**, choose **forward or reverse**, turn the **key on**, and **press the accelerator gently**. On an electric cart nothing happens until you press the pedal. On most gas carts, pressing the pedal starts the engine, and lifting off stops it again.

## Step by step

1. **Unplug the charger** (electric). Many carts won't move while it's connected.
2. **Tow/Run switch to Run** if your cart has one. It's usually under the seat. Tow mode disables the drive system.
3. **Sit in the driver's seat** and make sure everyone is seated.
4. **Hold the brake** and select forward (F) or reverse (R). Never change direction while moving.
5. **Turn the key on.** Keep your foot off the accelerator.
6. **Release the parking brake** (on many carts, pressing the brake pedal releases it).
7. **Press the accelerator slowly** and check your path.

## Electric vs gas: what to expect

| | Electric | Gas |
|---|---|---|
| Key on | Energises the controller. No sound | Readies ignition and starter |
| Press pedal | Motor turns, cart moves | Starter cranks the engine, then the clutch engages |
| Lift off pedal | Cart coasts or regenerates | On course-style carts the engine shuts off |
| Reverse | Motor spins the other way, often with a beeper | Gearbox selector, often with a beeper |

Never run a gas cart in a closed garage. Exhaust fumes build up quickly.

## If it won't start: electric

- **Charger still plugged in, or Tow mode on.** The two most common causes.
- **Flat or failing batteries.** Check pack voltage. Our [battery test guide](/blog/how-to-tell-if-a-golf-cart-battery-is-bad) shows how. If the charger also won't run, see [golf cart charger not working](/shop/golf-cart-charger-not-working).
- **Loose or corroded cables.** Check every terminal.
- **Solenoid.** Listen for a click when you press the pedal. No click (or a click but no movement) points to the solenoid or controller. See [how to test a golf cart solenoid](/blog/how-to-test-golf-cart-solenoid).
- **Key switch or F/R switch:** worn contacts can cut power intermittently.

## If it won't start: gas

- **Fuel:** is there fresh fuel in the tank, and is the cap vent clear?
- **Fuel delivery:** a cracked pulse line or weak pump starves the engine. See our [EZGO fuel pump guide](/shop/ezgo-golf-cart-fuel-pump-diagram-guide).
- **12 V starting battery:** gas carts still need a healthy 12 V battery to crank.
- **Spark plug and air filter:** cheap to replace and often neglected.
- **Pedal/micro switch:** on pedal-start carts, a faulty switch means pressing the pedal does nothing.

When in doubt, the owner's manual for your make and year (E-Z-GO, Club Car and Yamaha all differ slightly) has the exact sequence and safety interlocks.
